These terms of service govern the SMS notification program of OpenClaw ("the platform"), a self-hosted automation and monitoring platform that delivers automated operational notifications to authorized system administrators.

Description of Service

OpenClaw programmatically sends transactional SMS notifications to enrolled administrators. Messages include task completion notifications, system health alerts, scheduled reminders, and two-factor authentication codes. All messages are machine-generated by the platform's monitoring and workflow engine in response to system events, scheduled jobs, and authentication requests. The SMS program does not support conversational or person-to-person messaging.

SMS Enrollment Is Optional

Enrollment in SMS notifications is optional and is offered as a convenience. Administrators can use and access the platform without enrolling in SMS. Consent to receive SMS messages is never a condition of using the platform or completing any transaction. Where two-factor authentication is used, SMS is one available delivery method; alternate authentication methods are available, so SMS is not required for access.

SMS Messaging Program

By enrolling a mobile phone number through the platform's SMS enrollment form and confirming consent, an administrator agrees to receive automated SMS messages from the platform at that number.

  • Program name: OpenClaw Notifications
  • Message frequency: Variable, based on platform activity, configured workflows, and authentication events.
  • Message and data rates: Message and data rates may apply, as charged by the recipient's mobile carrier.
  • Help: Reply HELP to any message to receive support contact information.
  • Opt-out: Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe from all future SMS messages. Administrators may also opt out by removing their phone number from the platform's SMS settings.

Supported carriers include all major U.S. carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Administrators are responsible for ensuring that any phone number they enroll is their own and that they have the right to receive messages at that number. The platform does not enroll numbers on behalf of third parties and does not operate any public marketing list.
