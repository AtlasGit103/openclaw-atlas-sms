Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

OpenClaw, operated by Adam Melton, respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information collected through our SMS notification program.

Information We Collect

We collect the mobile phone number you provide when you sign up for SMS notifications through our sign-up form. We do not collect mobile numbers from any other source.

How We Use Your Information

Your mobile number is used solely to send the SMS notifications you signed up for, including task completion alerts, system status alerts, reminders, and verification codes.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information, including your mobile phone number, with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes at any time. SMS messages are delivered through Twilio Inc.; Twilio processes this data solely to deliver messages on our behalf, per the Twilio Privacy Notice.

Opt-In Is Optional

Signing up for SMS is optional. Consent to receive SMS messages is not required to use OpenClaw or to purchase any product or service.

Opt-Out

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. After opting out you will receive a one-time confirmation and no further messages. Reply HELP to any message for assistance. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Data Retention

We retain your mobile number only as long as needed to provide the notification service. You may request removal at any time by opting out or contacting us.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact Adam Melton at meltonshrugged@gmail.com.
