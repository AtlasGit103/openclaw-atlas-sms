Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

This privacy policy describes how OpenClaw ("the platform"), a self-hosted automation and monitoring platform, handles personal information in connection with SMS notifications sent to authorized system administrators.

Information Collected

The platform collects the mobile phone numbers of authorized system administrators who voluntarily enroll to receive automated SMS notifications. Phone numbers are provided directly by administrators through the platform's SMS enrollment form. Enrollment in SMS is optional and is not required to use the platform.

How Information Is Used

Enrolled phone numbers are used solely to deliver automated, machine-generated transactional SMS messages, including task completion notifications, system health alerts, scheduled reminders, and two-factor authentication codes. Messages are generated programmatically by the platform's monitoring and workflow engine in response to system events, scheduled jobs, and authentication requests.

SMS Is Optional

SMS notifications are an optional convenience feature. Administrators can use and access the platform without enrolling in SMS. Where two-factor authentication is used, SMS is one available delivery method among others; alternate authentication methods are available, so SMS enrollment is never required to access the platform or complete any action.

Information Sharing

No personal information is sold, rented, shared, or disclosed to any third party for marketing or any other purpose. SMS delivery is carried out by Twilio Inc. as the messaging provider. Twilio processes phone numbers and message content solely to deliver messages; Twilio's handling of this data is governed by the Twilio Privacy Notice. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes at any time.

Opt-Out and Help

Administrators may opt out of receiving SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message, or by removing their phone number from the platform's SMS settings. Replying HELP returns support contact information. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on platform activity and configured workflows.

Data Retention

Phone numbers and message logs are retained only as long as needed to operate the notification service. Logs may be purged or rotated according to the platform's retention configuration.

Security

The platform runs on a private virtual private server with access restricted to authorized administrators. Administrative access is secured via encrypted connections.

Changes to This Policy
